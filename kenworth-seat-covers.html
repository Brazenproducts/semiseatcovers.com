Kenworth trucks are built to a higher standard — and Kenworth drivers expect the same from their accessories. The W900 is the most iconic truck on the road; the T680 is the most efficient. Both deserve seat covers that fit right and last.

Kenworth W900 (1961-Present)

The W900 has been in production for over 60 years — the longest-running truck model in North America. Seat configurations vary enormously across decades. Make sure you know your seat manufacturer before ordering.

Kenworth T880 (2014-Present)
